How It Works

1. Buy a Laptop

Browse our collection of new and refurbished laptops. Place your order and enjoy fast, reliable delivery to your door.

2. Sell Your Laptop

List your pre-owned laptop. Once we receive it, our team refurbishes it and we handle delivery to the buyer.

3. Sell to Easytec

Get a valuation offer from us. We’ll collect the laptop via courier, inspect it, and finalize the payment after inspection.
